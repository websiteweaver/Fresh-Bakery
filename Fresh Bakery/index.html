The Art of Baking

Founded in 1998, Fresh Bakery started with a simple mission: to bring back the forgotten taste of real, slow-fermented bread. We don't use preservatives or shortcuts. Just flour, water, salt, and time.


Our bakers arrive at 3 AM every morning to ensure that the croissant you enjoy with your coffee was pulled from the oven just moments ago.
